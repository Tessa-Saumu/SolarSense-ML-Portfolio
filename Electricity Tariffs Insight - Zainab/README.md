# Electricity Tariff Analysis for SMEs in Sub-Saharan Africa ⚡📊

##  Project Overview

This project analyses electricity tariff disparities across selected Sub-Saharan African (SSA) countries and evaluates their cost impact on Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs).

The dashboard was built in **Power BI** and focuses on:

* Cross-country tariff comparison
* Monthly and annual SME electricity cost estimates
* Regional tariff trends
* Electricity price growth over time (2022–2026)

The goal is to support **energy investment decisions**, highlight **high-cost markets**, and show where alternative energy solutions (e.g., solar) are most economically viable.

---

##  Countries Included

The analysis provides regional balance across SSA:

* West Africa: Nigeria, Ghana
* East Africa: Kenya, Tanzania
* Central Africa: Cameroon, DR Congo
* Southern Africa: South Africa, Zambia

---

##  Dashboard Features

### 🔹 KPI Cards

* Highest electricity tariff

### 🔹 Time-Series Analysis (2022–2026)

* Tariff trends by country

##  Key Assumptions

* SME benchmark consumption: **500 kWh/month**
* Monthly bill = Tariff × Consumption

* Excel / CSV for data preparation

Electricity costs vary by more than **4× across the analysed markets**, with most countries experiencing **consistent tariff increases**, significantly affecting SME operating costs.
