The Problem: Energy Access Crisis for SSA SMEs

Across 47 Sub-Saharan African countries, unreliable electricity is the single greatest operational constraint for small and medium enterprises — driving up costs, destroying cold chain integrity, and locking SMEs out of global competitiveness.
