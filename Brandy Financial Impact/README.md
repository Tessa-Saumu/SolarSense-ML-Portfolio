README - Solar Energy Financial Dashboard
PROJECT OVERVIEW
Power BI dashboard analyzing solar investment viability for SMEs across 40+ Sub-Saharan African countries, helping stakeholders identify high-opportunity segments and regions.
DASHBOARD PAGES
Page 1: SME Financial Impact Analysis
Investment Required vs Annual Savings by Business Type
Financial Performance Table (ROI, payback, benefit level by country)
Distribution of Financial Benefit Levels
ROI vs Payback Period Scatter Plot
Page 2: Regional & Country Comparison
Solar Investment ROI Map Across Sub-Saharan Africa
Regional ROI Distribution
ROI Heat Map: Country vs Business Type
KPI Cards: Top Country, Top SME Type, Average Regional ROI
KEY METRICS
Average ROI: 33.96%
Top SME Type: Agriculture (36-47% ROI)
Top Country: South Sudan (47.16% ROI)
Average Payback: 2.88 years
Benefit Distribution: Very High (25%), High (67%), Moderate (8%)
DATA SOURCE
Dataset includes 40+ countries with four SME types:
Agriculture (Solar Water Pump)
Cold Storage (Solar Refrigeration)
Food Retail (Small SHS 3-49Wp)
Small Manufacturing (Larger SHS 50Wp+)
KEY FINDINGS
Agriculture delivers highest returns (36-47% ROI) in East/West Africa
South Sudan, Somalia, and Rwanda are top-performing countries
East Africa has highest concentration of "Very High" benefit cases
Average payback is under 3 years for best-performing segments
