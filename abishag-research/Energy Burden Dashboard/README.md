 # SME Energy Burden Dashboard – README
Overview

This Power BI dashboard calculates and visualizes the energy burden for SMEs, showing the percentage of monthly revenue spent on electricity. It also compares grid electricity costs vs projected solar costs over time for selected countries.

Data Sources

Electricity tariffs: Compiled by Zainab (electricity_tariff_csv and electricity_tariffs_timeseries_long)

SME revenue data: Used to calculate energy burden (% of revenue)

Visuals

Grid vs Solar (Clustered Column Chart)

X-axis: Country

Y-axis: Estimated_Monthly_Bill_USD, Solar_Cost_USD

Compares monthly costs of grid vs solar electricity

Tariff per Country (Clustered Column Chart)

X-axis: Country

Y-axis: Tariff_USD_per_kWh

Shows electricity tariffs for each country

Time Series (Line Chart)

X-axis: Year

Y-axis: Annual_Cost, Solar_Annual_Cost

Legend: Country

Shows trends in annual grid and solar costs over time

Key Insights

Solar adoption is increasing, but grid electricity remains widely used.

Kenya has the highest electricity costs and energy burden; DRC and Nigeria show lower usage and burden.

Countries with high tariffs present the greatest potential savings from solar adoption.
