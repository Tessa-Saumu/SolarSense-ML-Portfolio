What this prototype can and cannot establish

The consumption model is trained on US commercial-building survey data; its accuracy has not been validated on Nairobi small businesses. Solar estimates use irradiance-derived targets, not measured rooftop output. Cost assumptions are illustrative. This is a demonstrator of the assessment workflow, not a quotation, a financing recommendation or a production design.

The Nairobi monthly baseline is the default because it beat the solar XGBoost model on the retained city test. A separate consumption model runs for your inputs; the two models solve different parts of the assessment.
